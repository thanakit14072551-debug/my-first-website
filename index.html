Action

  • The God of High School

    The God of High School follows Jin Mori, a carefree fighter who joins a national tournament to test his skills. What begins as a simple martial arts competition quickly turns into a battle involving mysterious powers, ancient legends, and god-like beings. With intense fight scenes and fast pacing, the anime delivers non-stop action.

    Why watch: Fast-paced fights and insane animation.

    Rating:★★★★☆ (7.9/10)

  • Hell’s Paradise

    Hell’s Paradise follows Gabimaru, a deadly ninja sentenced to death, who is sent to a mysterious island to find the elixir of immortality. Alongside his executioner, Sagiri, he must survive deadly creatures and uncover the island’s dark secrets.

    Why watch: Dark story, intense fights, and a mix of horror and action.

    Rating:★★★★★ (8.5/10)

Romance

  • The Angel Next Door Spoils Me Rotten

    The Angel Next Door Spoils Me Rotten follows Amane, a lonely high school boy whose life changes when his kind and beautiful neighbor, Mahiru, begins taking care of him. As they spend more time together, their relationship slowly grows into something deeper.

    Why watch: Wholesome romance, relaxing story, and strong character chemistry.

    Rating:★★★★☆ (7.8/10)

  • The Fragrant Flower Blooms with Dignity

    The Fragrant Flower Blooms with Dignity follows Rintaro, a kind but misunderstood delinquent, and Kaoruko, a gentle and refined girl from a prestigious school. Despite coming from completely different worlds, the two slowly grow closer, challenging the stereotypes around them.

    Why watch: Heartwarming romance, strong character development, and a refreshing take on “opposites attract.”

    Rating:★★★★★ (8.8/10)

Drama

  • Violet Evergarden

    Violet Evergarden follows Violet, a former soldier who struggles to understand human emotions after the war. She becomes an "Auto Memory Doll," writing letters for others, and through these experiences, she begins to learn the meaning of love and the last words given to her.

    Why watch: Beautiful animation, emotional storytelling, and deep character development.

    Rating:★★★★★ (9.0/10)

  • A Silent Voice

    A Silent Voice tells the story of Shoya, a former bully who seeks redemption after tormenting a deaf girl named Shoko in elementary school. Years later, he tries to make amends and face the consequences of his past actions.

    Why watch: Powerful message about bullying, redemption, and mental health with emotional impact.

    Rating:★★★★★ (9.1/10)

Sports

  • Uma Musume Pretty Derby

    Uma Musume Pretty Derby takes place in a world where famous racehorses are reborn as girls with incredible speed. Special Week, an aspiring runner, enrolls in an academy to compete in high-stakes races and achieve her dream of becoming the best.

    Why watch: Energetic races, inspiring competition, and surprisingly emotional character stories.

    Rating:★★★★☆ (8.2/10)

  • Haikyuu!!

    Haikyuu!! follows Hinata, a determined high school student who dreams of becoming a great volleyball player despite his short height. Joining his school team, he works together with his former rival Kageyama to compete and grow stronger.

    Why watch: Hype matches, strong teamwork, and motivating character growth.

    Rating:★★★★★ (9.0/10)
